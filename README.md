### What I do differently

Most AI systems tell you what you want to hear. Mine are built to do the opposite: every conclusion is attacked by an independent auditor before it reaches the user.

- **Adversarial verification.** One agent verifies that every claim has real evidence. Another attacks the conclusions and looks for contradictions. The premise is that the analysis is wrong until proven otherwise.
- **Explicit confidence.** Every data point carries its source, its date and its confidence level.
- **No invented data.** What can't be confirmed is declared as unconfirmed. A report that admits its gaps is worth more than one that hides them.

### What I've built

**IRIS SCE** — A domain-agnostic deliberative reasoning engine. Parallel agents with distinct roles, adversarial auditing, credibility scoring and a coherence lock that prevents a report from contradicting itself.

**A commercial intelligence system in production** — Multi-agent company research, a conversational agent across four channels, and multi-instance administration with isolated deployments per client.
