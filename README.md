## What I do differently

Most AI systems tell you what you want to hear. Mine are built to do the opposite: **every conclusion is attacked by an independent auditor before it reaches the user.**

- **Adversarial verification.** One agent verifies that every claim has real evidence. Another attacks the conclusions and hunts for contradictions. The premise is that the analysis is wrong until proven otherwise.
- **Explicit confidence.** Every data point carries its source, its date and its confidence level.
- **No invented data.** What can't be confirmed is declared as unconfirmed — never filled in. A report that admits its gaps is worth more than one that hides them.
- **A coherence lock.** A report can't state one thing on its cover and conclude another. It's enforced by design, not by policy.

This isn't a feature. It's the architecture.

## Work

**IRIS SCE — Swarm Cognitive Engine**
A domain-agnostic deliberative reasoning engine. Parallel agents with distinct postures — produce, verify, challenge, consolidate — adversarial auditing, credibility scoring capped by evidence, and a coherence lock. The domain enters from the outside as configuration: the engine itself knows nothing about any business.

**Commercial intelligence platform — in production**
Multi-agent company research producing audited 20-page dossiers, a conversational agent across four channels (messaging, email, voice, SMS), automated commercial lifecycle, credit metering, and multi-instance administration with an isolated deployment per client. Built solo.

**CERBERUS — deterministic verification**
A verification and audit engine designed as a pure reducer: immutable claim ledger, explicit command-and-event protocol, deterministic scheduler with a monotonic progress token. The domain never enters the core.

**CASANDRA — discovery**
Non-deterministic by design. A heterogeneous, divergent swarm that generates candidate hypotheses for CERBERUS to verify. The two engines are opposites on purpose: one explores, the other proves.

**Applied systems**
Signal-driven prospecting for commercial real estate, opportunity alerting for industrial suppliers, and vertical intelligence products built on the same engine with different data layers.
